Biography

Draft

Born in Çameli, Denizli, in 1961, he is a lawyer, author and presenter who graduated from Istanbul University’s Faculty of Law. He has worked in law, publishing and management, and has provided commentary on couplets from classical poetry on the TRT program “Can Veren Pervaneler” (Moths That Give Their Lives). He continues his work as an independent author and speaker and presents “Conversations with Hayati” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. He specializes in classical Turkish literature and commentary on couplets from divan poetry. His published books include the “Can Veren Pervaneler” (Moths That Give Their Lives) series, “Hayati Meseleler” (Matters of Life) and “Hayati Dengeler” (Balances in Life).
