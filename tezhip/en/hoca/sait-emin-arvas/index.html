Biography

Draft

Born in Gürpınar, Van, in 1960, he is a theologian who graduated from Van Imam Hatip High School and Erzurum’s Higher Islamic Institute. He retired after serving under the Presidency of Religious Affairs as district mufti in Akyazı and Adapazarı and as deputy provincial mufti of Sakarya. In 1999, he prepared his thesis at Marmara University, titled “Kur'an ve Sünnette Ehl-i Beyt Kavramı” (The Concept of Ahl al-Bayt in the Quran and Sunnah). He gives talks on hadith, the Sunnah and family ethics, and presents “From Hadith to Life” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel.
