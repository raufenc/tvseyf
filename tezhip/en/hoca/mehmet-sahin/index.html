Mehmet Şahin

Born in Yusufeli, Artvin, in 1984, he is a researcher, author and businessman. He earned his undergraduate degree in Political Science and International Relations at Istanbul University’s Faculty of Political Sciences, followed by a master’s degree and doctorate at Istanbul Arel University. He chairs GOAT Group’s board and presents “World Politics and Current Affairs” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His fields include political science, international relations, world politics and regional geopolitics. His books include “Hitler Tehdidi”, “Akdeniz Jeopolitiği ve Enerjinin Geleceği” and “Istırap Dolu Bir Tarih: Ahıska Türkleri”.
