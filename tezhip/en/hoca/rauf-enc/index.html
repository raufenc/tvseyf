Biography

Draft

An educator and author, he graduated from the Department of Electronics and Communications Engineering at Yıldız Technical University and the Faculty of Theology at Ankara University. As an independent educator, he develops digital education projects, writes for the Medyamit platform and presents “Readings from the Maktubat” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His areas of expertise are Islamic studies, disciplining the self, neuroscience and digital learning design. His published works include “NöroTerbiye: İçimizdeki Düşmanı Dosta Çevirmek” (Neurodiscipline: Turning the Enemy Within into a Friend), the “İyiliklerle Büyüyorum” (Growing Through Good Deeds) coloring books for children and “Osmanlı Padişahları Kart Seti” (Ottoman Sultans Card Set).
