Biography

Draft

Born in Yusufeli, Artvin, in 1984, he is a researcher, author and businessman who earned his undergraduate degree in the Department of Political Science and International Relations at Istanbul University’s Faculty of Political Sciences and his master’s degree in the Department of International Relations at Istanbul Arel University. He chairs GOAT Group’s board and presents “World Politics and Current Affairs” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His areas of expertise are political science, international relations, world politics and regional geopolitics. His published books include “Hitler Tehdidi” (The Hitler Threat), “Akdeniz Jeopolitiği ve Enerjinin Geleceği” (Mediterranean Geopolitics and the Future of Energy) and “Istırap Dolu Bir Tarih: Ahıska Türkleri” (A History of Suffering: The Meskhetian Turks).
