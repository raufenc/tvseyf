Biography

Draft

A theologian and author, he completed his doctorate in Basic Islamic Sciences at Istanbul Sabahattin Zaim University and serves as a preacher at the Sancaktepe District Mufti’s Office in Istanbul under the Presidency of Religious Affairs. He works in Islamic law (fiqh) and examined the Fatawa al-Hindiyya in his doctoral thesis. He writes for the Medyamit platform and presents “Important Issues” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His published book is “Gençlere Fikir Dağarcığı” (Repertory of Thought), which has also been published in English under the title “Repertory of Thought”.
