Biography

Draft

Born in Bafra, Samsun, in 1959, he is a journalist and author who writes for the Medyamit news website after working for Türkiye newspaper, TGRT and various advertising agencies. His work focuses on Türkiye’s recent social and economic history and period novels, and he presents “Straight Talk” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His published books are “Irmağın Kıyısındaki Köy: Bengü” (The Village by the River: Bengü), “Öğlen Karanlığı” (Darkness at Noon) and “Tünelin Ortasında” (In the Middle of the Tunnel).
