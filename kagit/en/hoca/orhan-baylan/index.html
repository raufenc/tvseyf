Orhan Baylan

Born in Bafra, Samsun, in 1959, he is a journalist and author. After working for Türkiye newspaper, TGRT and various advertising agencies, he now works as a manager and writer at the Medyamit news website. His work concerns Türkiye’s recent social and economic history and historical fiction. He presents “Straight Talk” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His published books are “Irmağın Kıyısındaki Köy Bengü”, “Öğlen Karanlığı” and “Tünelin Ortasında”.
