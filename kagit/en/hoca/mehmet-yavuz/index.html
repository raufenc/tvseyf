Biography

Draft

He graduated from Atatürk University’s Faculty of Medicine and completed specialist training in its Neurology Department. He works as a neurologist at the REEM Neuropsychiatry Center, which he founded in Levent, Istanbul, in 1997. His fields include neurology, neuropsychiatry, degenerative brain disorders and magnetic stimulation. He writes for Medyamit and presents “Secrets of the Brain” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His books include “Beynin Sırları ve Manyetik Şifreleri”, “Ben Bilmem Beyin Bilir” and “Ebeveyn Okulu”.
