Hayati İnanç

Born in Çameli, Denizli, in 1961, he is a lawyer, author and presenter who graduated from Istanbul University’s Faculty of Law. He has worked in law, publishing, management and broadcasting, including at TRT and Diyanet TV. He continues as an independent author and speaker and appears in “Conversations with Hayati” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His specialty is classical Turkish literature and commentary on divan poetry. His books include the “Can Veren Pervaneler” series, “Hayati Meseleler” and “Hayati Dengeler”.
