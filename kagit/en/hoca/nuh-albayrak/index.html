Biography

Draft

Born in Konya in 1960, he is a journalist, media executive and author. After many years at Türkiye newspaper, he works as editor-in-chief and columnist at Star. His fields include journalism, media management, recent Turkish political history and the late Ottoman period. He produces and presents “Passage of Truths” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His books include “İçten Dıştan Entrikalar”, “Devlet Yıkan Tefrikalar” and “Darbeden Beter Vesayetler”.
