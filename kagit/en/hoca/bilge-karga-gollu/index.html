Bilge Karga Göllü

Born in Pınarhisar, Kırklareli, in 1986, she is an academic and poet. She is a faculty member in Classical Turkish Literature within Çukurova University’s Turkish Language and Literature Department and vice dean of its Faculty of Arts and Sciences. Her work covers classical Turkish literature, Persian texts and divan poetry commentary. She appears alongside Nihat Öztoprak in episodes of “From Couplet to Heart” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. Her books include the study “Ömer Necmî Efendi Farsça Dîvânçe (İnceleme-Metin)” and the poetry collections “Ömrü Beş Geçe” and “Alt Üst Şiirler”.
