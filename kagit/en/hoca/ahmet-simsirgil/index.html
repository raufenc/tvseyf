Biography

Draft

Born in Boyabat, Sinop, in 1959, he is a historian and academic teaching early modern history in the History Department of Marmara University’s Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences. He specializes in Ottoman history, early modern history and Ottoman urban and provincial administration. He produces and presents “History and Contemplation” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His books include the “Kayı” and “Otağ” series and “Valide Sultanlar ve Harem”.
