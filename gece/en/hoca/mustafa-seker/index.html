Biography

Draft

An academic and author, he holds a professorship in the Social Studies Education division of the Department of Social Sciences and Turkish Education at Yıldız Technical University’s Faculty of Education. He specializes in social studies education, history teaching and values education, writes for the Medyamit platform and presents “Education and Civilization” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His published books include “Değer Sizseniz Değer Sizsiniz” (If You Are the Value, You Are Valuable) and “Cihan Hükümdarlarından Yarınlara Mektuplar” (Letters from World Rulers to the Future).
