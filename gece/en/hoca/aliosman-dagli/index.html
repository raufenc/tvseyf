Dr.

Aliosman Dağlı

A theologian and author, he completed his doctorate in Basic Islamic Sciences at Istanbul Sabahattin Zaim University and works as a preacher at the Sancaktepe District Mufti’s Office in Istanbul under the Presidency of Religious Affairs. His fields include basic Islamic sciences, Islamic law, creed and Sufism (tasawwuf). He writes for Medyamit and presents “Important Issues” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His books include “Gençler İçin Fikir Dağarcığı” and “Lütuf Yolu: Kur’an ve Sünnetin Işığında Mânevî Hayat”.
