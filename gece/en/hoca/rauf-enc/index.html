Biography

Draft

An educator and author, he graduated in Electronics and Communications Engineering at Yıldız Technical University and in Theology at Ankara University. As an independent educator, he develops digital education projects, writes for Medyamit and presents “Readings from the Maktubat” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His fields include Islamic studies, disciplining the self, neuroscience and digital learning design. His published book is “NöroTerbiye: İçimizdeki Düşmanı Dosta Çevirmek”.
