Biography

Draft

Born in Vezirköprü, Samsun, in 1960, he is a literary historian and academic serving as faculty member and head of the Turkish Language and Literature Department in the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences at Fatih Sultan Mehmet Vakıf University. He specializes in classical Turkish literature, Ottoman dynastic poets, text editing and divan poetry commentary. He produces and presents “From Couplet to Heart” on Seyf TV’s YouTube channel. His books include “Sâhib-Kırân-ı Zaman Yavuz Sultan Selîm Han”, “Refî-i Âmidî – Cân u Cânân” and “Ramazan-nâme”.
